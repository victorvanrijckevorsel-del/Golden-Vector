# Holistic cross-phase review — resolutions (Capture & Behaviour engine, Phases 0–4)

Date: 2026-06-17
Method: adversarial multi-agent Workflow (5 dimensions — cross-phase-consistency, honesty-end-to-end,
architecture-one-copy, integration-runtime, completeness-critic — 23 agents, every finding
independently verified by a second agent). 18 raw → **15 confirmed** (1 HIGH, 6 MEDIUM, 5 LOW, 3 NIT).

The 15 confirmed findings collapse to **6 distinct issues** — the staleness gap, the trend-horizon
drift and the cutoff drift were each surfaced 3–4× by different dimensions (which is itself the
signal that they were the real ones). All 6 are resolved on `dev-vic`.
